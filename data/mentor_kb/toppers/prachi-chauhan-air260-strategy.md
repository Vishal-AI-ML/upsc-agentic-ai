# Prachi Chauhan (AIR 260, Hindi Medium) — Strategy Notes

> High-value mentor knowledge. Present these as "a Hindi-medium AIR 260 topper's approach", never quote the raw interview or name the source unless asked.

## Profile & context
- Gaon (Jahad, Vidisha, MP) se, Hindi medium, science background (BSc Maths).
- 4th attempt me AIR 260. Pehle 2 baar Mains tak pahunchi par 2-10 marks se reh jaati thi.
- Is baar ka game-changer: **GS me ~455 marks** (top-10 rank ke aas-paas), jabki Hindi medium me GS ko normally low-scoring maana jaata hai.
- Belief: Hindi medium ab bhi GS me top marks laa sakta hai; barrier AI + online resources se lagbhag khatam.

## Daily routine (self-study, ghar se)
- 7:30 uthna; ghar ke sabse upar wala shaant kamra study ke liye.
- Slots: subah 10-11 tak, phir 1:30-5:00, phir 7:30 se raat 1-2 baje tak (jab topic acche chalein).
- Distraction control: WhatsApp mostly band; Instagram/Facebook pe active nahi. Sirf Telegram + YouTube study material ke liye. "Jab tak success nahi, social media nahi."

## GS gap kaise bhara (core answer-writing shift)
1. **Question ki demand samajhna** — agar 2 part hain to dono ko balance do; ek part ko zyada kheechna marks nahi badhata.
2. **Multidimensional answers** — ek question ko doosre GS papers se link karo (Society ko Polity/Disaster Mgmt/Ethics se).
3. **Points + example** — 10-marker me 6-7 points; har point ke saath ek example. Do-part me 4-4 ya 5-5 points.
4. **Data + examples** khoob bhar ke.

## Answer-writing (exam craft)
- **Pehle 5 questions bahut acche likho** → examiner ke mann me positive impression set ho jaata hai, phir beech ke average answers bhi thoda upar assess hote hain. (Strategy: Q1-5 best, phir Q11-20, phir beech ke average.)
- **Copy end tak bharna** (Shakti Dubey ki copies se seekha — har answer bilkul last tak).
- Speed ke liye **practice** — jo test me likha wahi exam me replicate hota hai (pen tak same rakha ek baar).

## Question decoding
- Jo poocha hai wahi **heading** banao (e.g. "Bhukamp ke kaaran", "Bhukamp ka prabhav") taaki tez-checking examiner ko dikhe ki demand samjhi.
- Galti jo log karte hain: "jo poocha" nahi, "jo aata hai" likhte hain. Extra knowledge sirf **baad ke points (6th-7th)** me daalo, shuruaat demand se.

## Intro & Conclusion (paper-wise)
- **GS1**: background/historical se intro. **GS2**: judgment/article se intro. **GS3**: data se intro. **GS4**: philosopher quote se intro (agar demand "explain" nahi hai to definition ki jagah quote).
- **Conclusion**: hamesha 2-3 way-forward — SDG se link / govt scheme se link / committee suggestion se link. GS2 me judgment ke saath conclude.

## Paper-specific
### GS3 (uniqueness)
- Data ki demand zyada — har alternate question me kuch data daalo.
- Interlinking scope zyada (Disaster↔Geography, Sci-Tech↔Cyber Security).
- Short notes pehle se ready rakho (Prelims-Mains gap chhota hota hai). Har syllabus term pe 1-2 page note: heading + sub-heading me 8-10 points + example.
- Multi-source: ek basic source (definition) + coaching material/websites/AI se points add — sab ek hi jagah compile.
- PYQ se theme pakdo (e.g. Cyber Security har alternate year).

### GS1
- Is baar points-wise likha (pehle paragraph/conceptual likhti thi).
- GS3 pattern se link (Geography ko Disaster Mgmt style).
- Diagrams use kiye (e.g. Chandel/temple architecture ka design). Diagram/flowchart sirf tabhi aata hai jab pehle se practice ho.

### Ethics (84 → 119 marks)
- **Quote questions**: purane 10-12 saal ke quotes break karo, dimensions me todo — Political/Social/Economic YA Vyakti/Samuday/Rashtra/Vishwa. Isse quote answer bhi normal multi-point answer jaisa ban jaata hai.
- **Case studies**: current real example se intro (e.g. lockdown me CM apni maa ke antim sanskaar nahi ja paaye → civil servant ke samaksh challenges). Case ko GS1/2/3 se link (trafficking↔Geography golden triangle, Sci-Tech innovation, civil society).
- **Keywords**: conclusion me integrity/empathy/etc. daalna zaroori; ya philosopher principle (Utilitarianism, Kant ka Deontology, Rawls) se link.
- **Conclusion with judgment**: e.g. integrity pe Manoj Narayan case, transparency pe live-streaming (Swapnil Tripathi) case.
- **Examples multidimensional**: sirf civil servants/leaders nahi — daily-life + personal + govt schemes (e.g. PRAGATI platform e-governance me) bhi.

## Current Affairs
- Shuruaat me daily newspaper (The Hindu) — base banata hai; interview me bhi kaam aata hai.
- Baad ke attempts (base strong): newspaper sirf sarsari, + daily coaching CA video + ek monthly magazine + saal-end compilation.
- **Alag se CA notes mat banao** — static notes me har topic ke aage ek page khaali chhodo, aur relevant current point wahi add karo. Revision ek hi jagah se.

## Prelims
- Challenge: random/unpredictable questions; CSAT (2023 spike) ab real threat.
- **Ideal attempt**: GS ~85-90; CSAT ~50-55. Apni accuracy khud jaano (zyada attempt = negative).
- **3-reading test technique**: 1st reading (45-50 min) sirf pukhta questions (~30-35). 2nd reading — subconscious se aur link hote hain (~60-65 tak). 3rd (10-15 min) — thoda-bhi-link/educated guess.
- **Elimination** ka role ab bhi (2 tak pahunch gaye to chance zyada).
- Prelims se 15-20 din pehle sirf **PYQ** — UPSC extreme banaye to galat hone ke chance samajh aate hain; last 10-ish questions me madad.
- Test count attempts ke saath ghata: 1st attempt 50-60 → 2nd 40 → 3rd 20-25 → 4th sirf 5-6 full-length + purane revisit.

### CSAT
- Concept + question language samajho, calculation pe kam. PYQ ka sabse bada role.
- UPSC ek portion tough karke doosra easy banata hai — kisi ek ke bharose mat jao.
- Maths+Reasoning **pehle** solve (shuruaat ke 45-50 min), phir comprehension. 1 question pe 1 min se zyada nahi.

## NCERT / basic books
- Last 1 month: 10-13 ghante (8 GS + 4 CSAT); 2 ghante daily PYQ.
- Last month me test-series ki jagah **basic books/NCERT** revise — summary nahi, moolbook se (questions ki language book jaisi hoti hai).
- **Kaunsi NCERT**: History 6-7-8 (Hamare Ateet 1-2-3) + 12th (+ 11th Art&Culture); world-history/Mongol wale chapters skip. Geography 11-12 zaroori, par 6-10 se bhi questions aaye — 8th se cover karo. Sci-Tech 9-10 (11-12 advanced). Biology ka Environment section acha.
- NCERT 80% Prelims ke liye; Mains ke liye NCERT ki **bhasha/storytelling** answer-intro sikhaati hai + model examples (e.g. Bolivia primary health).

## Interview (pehla, cordial experience)
- **Golden period = Mains ke baad ke 2-2.5 mahine** (result se pehle). Prachi ki galti: interview prep sirf result ke baad shuru ki → home-state (MP/Bhopal) questions miss.
- PR group (mentor-banwaya) + mocks se personality + nervousness improve.
- Board cordial tha; questions mostly background se (kisan pita, housewife maa, optional). Nahi aata to bol do.
- Check karta hai: **soch kaise hai + communication + personality** (knowledge kam).
- Early se: language/vocabulary theek karo ("karunga/karungi" gender), newspaper reading habit.

## Women aspirants (challenges + message)
- Challenges: patriarchy (ladki ko pehle "prove" karna padta hai), security concern (bahar bhejne ka darr), internet/resource awareness gap, bachpan se kamzor base.
- Message **parents ko**: ladkiyon ko ladko ke barabar resources + investment do; failure pe confidence banaye rakho.
- Message **ladkiyon ko**: opportunity mile to 100% use karo — apne aur poori women-community ke liye.

## Setbacks & motivation
- Strong **"WHY"** rakho — Prachi ka why: gaon ki har ladki ke liye example set karna. Failure jhelne ki taakat isse aati hai.
- Biggest pillar: **maa** — anpadh hote hue bhi hamesha support, "nishkaam bhaav se kaam", "apna kaam imaandari se karo, result aayega".

## Final message
- Hawa-hawai motivation nahi — **discipline > motivation**. Success rate ~0.25%.
- Strong WHY + sahi strategy + guidance → itne saare attempts na dene padein. Discipline rakho, motivation apne aap aata hai.
